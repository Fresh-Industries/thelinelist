**Date:** Friday, Aug 21, 2026 (America/Chicago)

**Method:** Public pages fetched Aug 21, 2026; web-search ranking observations (not a logged-in Google SERP screenshot); published media kits and vendor-claimed numbers only. **No invented search volumes.** Unpublished volumes are marked **UNKNOWN**.

## 0. Bottom line

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|

## 1. CoPack Connect — what the product actually is

**URLs reviewed:** [homepage](https://copackconnect.com/), [visibility-plans](https://copackconnect.com/visibility-plans/), [about](https://copackconnect.com/about/), [FAQ](https://copackconnect.com/frequently-asked-questions/), [manufacturers](https://copackconnect.com/manufacturers/), [CPA launch post](https://copackconnect.com/news/copack-connect-cpa-exclusive-rfq-tool/), product pages ([hot fill](https://copackconnect.com/contract-packaging/hot-fill-bottling/), [retorting](https://copackconnect.com/contract-packaging/retorting/), [stick pack](https://copackconnect.com/contract-packaging/stick-pack-dry-packaging/), [chocolate chip cookies](https://copackconnect.com/food/chocolate-chip-cookies-contract-manufacturer/)), paid plant page ([MSW Packaging](https://copackconnect.com/manufacturer-page/msw-packaging-inc/), [CTI](https://copackconnect.com/manufacturer-page/cti/)), thin listing ([Glen Copack](https://copackconnect.com/parking-spaces/glen-copack/), [LiDestri](https://copackconnect.com/parking-spaces/lidestri-food-drink/), [The Fresh Factory](https://copackconnect.com/parking-spaces/the-fresh-factory/), [Fantasy Cookie](https://copackconnect.com/parking-spaces/fantasy-cookie-co/)).

### Product

CoPack Connect is a **logged-in AI-RFQ matchmaking marketplace**, not a public plant directory.

- Built by **The Melville Group (TMG)**; owner named as Carl Melville.
- Launched **Nov 3, 2024 at Pack Expo International** as a beta ([CPA / TMG announcement](https://copackconnect.com/news/copack-connect-cpa-exclusive-rfq-tool/); also covered by [Packaging World](https://www.packworld.com/leaders-new/business-drivers-specialty/contract-packaging/article/22930298/aidriven-copackconnect-platform-matches-brands-with-external-manufacturers) and [Commercial Baking](https://commercialbaking.com/new-platform-connects-brands-and-co-manufacturers/)).
- **Exclusively endorsed by the Contract Packaging Association (CPA)** — site copy: “the only RFQ tool endorsed by the Association of Contract Manufacturers and Packagers.”
- FAQ: *“CoPack Connect is neither a manufacturer nor a broker.”* No commission. Direct connect after match, then they “get out of the way.”
- Brand flow: register → submit RFQ/project brief → AI matches against manufacturer capabilities → responses appear on a private dashboard → brand chooses who to reveal identity to. Commercial Baking reports matches typically in **24–48 hours**; FAQ says “a few days.”
- RFQ inputs called out on homepage/FAQ: product type and category, packaging format, estimated production volume, target launch date, ingredient/formulation requirements, certifications (organic, gluten-free, etc.), unique specs.
- Categories named on FAQ: snacks and confectionery, beverages, dietary supplements, personal care, pet products, “and more.” Not food-only.
- Claimed network size is inconsistent on-site: homepage **“3000+ Manufacturers”**; product pages **“2000+ Manufacturers.”** Launch copy said “thousands of detailed product profiles and manufacturers.” Treat as vendor claim, not an audited count.
- Testimonials on homepage are **anonymous titles** (Emerging Brand CEO, Leading Brand Director Commercialization, Private Label VP BD, Contract Manufacturer VP BD). No named brand endorsements on the pages fetched.

### Indexed plant pages vs logged-in RFQ

Two public plant URL patterns exist:

1. **`/manufacturer-page/{slug}/`** — paid “dedicated custom landing page” (Level 2+). Example: [MSW Packaging](https://copackconnect.com/manufacturer-page/msw-packaging-inc/) (3 Indiana plants, SQF, gluten-free/organic/kosher, VFFS/premade pouches, RFQ CTA + website). [CTI](https://copackconnect.com/manufacturer-page/cti/) (97,000 sq ft Bristol, IN contract packager). These look like manufacturer-authored copy.
2. **`/parking-spaces/{slug}/`** — thin auto-generated listings. Examples: Glen Copack (Watkins Glen, NY; address listed), LiDestri (“Address: Not available”), The Fresh Factory (Chicago address), Fantasy Cookie Co. (Los Angeles). Copy is short “Who we are” + capabilities + hard CTA: *“Take your brand to the next level with a full manufacturer account.”* Quality is uneven: Carbon Activated Corporation (activated carbon, Compton CA) is indexed under parking-spaces and is **not a food co-packer**.

`robots.txt` does **not** noindex plant pages. It allows crawl except `/wp/wp-admin/` and a plugin JSON path. Sitemap: `https://copackconnect.com/sitemap_index.xml` (fetch timed out; existence confirmed by robots.txt).

**Screenshot-level note — can Google find a specific plant via CoPack Connect?**

- **Yes, some plants are findable as CoPack Connect URLs.** A web search for MSW Packaging returned the CoPack Connect manufacturer-page as an indexed result. Parking-spaces for Glen Copack, LiDestri, Fresh Factory, and Fantasy Cookie also returned as indexed URLs.
- **No, Google will not use CoPack Connect as the way to find “the HPP plant in Meriden” or “sauce co-packer in NJ.”** Process queries return **the plants’ own sites** (Universal Pure, InnoMark, Chelten House, Berner, Thermal Kitchen, etc.). CoPack Connect did not appear in those process SERPs in this pass.
- **Practical SEO shape:** a thin public encyclopedia of product/process pages + a sparse set of paid plant landing pages + a larger set of parking-spaces, all funnelling to login/RFQ. It is **not** an SEO-indexed, process-sliced directory of every plant.

Quality issues a challenger can exploit:

- Product pages are generic process explainers with **no named plant table**, no MOQs, no line types, no state map.
- Cookie-dough page contains a **copy-paste error** about Tabasco-style sauces (fetched Aug 21, 2026).
- Manufacturer count disagrees with itself (2,000 vs 3,000).
- Parking-spaces include non-food and missing addresses.
- Testimonials are untitled-company quotes.

## 3. Adjacent directories — what they are and where they are weak

### 3PL Hub — [Find a Co-Manufacturer](https://www.3plhub.co/resources/tools/find-a-co-manufacturer)

- **1,096** facilities claimed; **51 states + 14 countries**; **244+ specialty tags**; **5 dietary certifications**.
- Public, **no signup**, live filter by country / US state / specialty tag; results load 25 at a time. Contact info on cards. Free to list.
- Data “compiled from co-manufacturer websites, brand-of-record filings, industry directories, and public production capacity disclosures.”
- **Weaknesses:** Side product of a 3PL matching business, not a food-process authority. Mixes food with beauty/supplements/pet. Specialty tags are keyword-ish (“sauces,” “gummies,” “kombucha”) not true process (retort vs hot fill vs HPP vs acidified). No claimed on-site audits. Depth is a card, not a plant dossier (MOQ, line speed, allergen suite, USDA vs FDA). Easy to out-depth on process pages.

### comanufacturers.com

- Positions as a **search engine / sourcing app** for North American CMs. Claims procurement teams at Kellogg’s, Kraft Heinz, McCain’s, US Foods use it (vendor claim, unverified here).
- Public preview: **6,588 matching manufacturers** with most of the list **locked** (“Website & contact details in the app”). Category chips show Food 1,934 / Beverages 1,139 etc. (preview UI).
- Profiles (in-app, per marketing copy): address, website, products, services, technical capabilities, contact, often capacity / certs / MOQ.
- **Weaknesses:** Login wall is an SEO ceiling — Google cannot rank what it cannot crawl. Breadth over food-process depth. Not a newsletter brand. If the public pages stay teaser cards, a fully public process directory can outrank them for HPP/retort/hot fill.

### Wonnda — [wonnda.com](https://wonnda.com/)

- Berlin-based global private-label / CM marketplace. Claims **20,000+ vetted manufacturers**, **25,000+ brands**, **600+ categories**, **41 countries**. “80% of the core contract manufacturers are in Europe, with a growing US network” ([contract manufacturers page](https://wonnda.com/contract-manufacturers/), updated June 4, 2026).
- **Pricing:** free for buyers and suppliers, no commission. Buyer upgrade **€39/month**. Supplier PRO (price not published on pricing page).
- Public supplier directory is real and indexed; many listings are EU cosmetics/supplements/fashion. Filters: category, country, certs, supplier type, MOQ (marketing copy).
- **Weaknesses:** Not a US food-process directory. US food plants are a minority. Trust scores and EU private-label SKUs will not satisfy a founder searching “retort pouch co-packer Midwest.” Magazine content (e.g. co-packer vs CM) is generic.

### GrowinCo — [growinco.com](https://growinco.com/)

- CPG co-man / sourcing platform: idle capacity listing, RFQs, consortium buying, project tracking. Claims **“+85k CPG players.”**
- **Weaknesses:** Capacity-marketplace, not an SEO directory. No evidence in this pass of public process-sliced plant pages ranking. “85k players” is a network claim, not a food-plant census. Logged-in workflow.

### GourmetPro shortlists — [2026 US food CM shortlist](http://gourmetpro.co/blog/food-contract-manufacturers-in-the-us-2026-shortlist)

- Editorial **60+ US food CMs/co-packers** for 2026 (national scale vs regional/mid-market). Names plants and processes (one snippet cites a CA facility with frozen / liquid fill / dry blend / retort / HPP; Universal Pure-class HPP/rotary fill). Explicitly “not an endorsement.”
- Core business is **paid market-entry / distributor-partner search**, not a free directory ([partner search](https://www.gourmetpro.co/services/distributor-partner-search/)).
- **Weaknesses:** A blog post, not a queryable database. No filters, no MOQ table, no ongoing plant CRM. Consultative lead-gen. Can be out-productized by a living directory; the prose quality is a content bar to beat, not a product moat.

### ThomasNet — food contract manufacturing / co-packing

- Industrial sourcing incumbent (125+ years). **2,500+ US co-packing suppliers** claimed. Filter by location/certs. Separate organic / plant-based category URLs.
- **Weaknesses:** Cross-industry sludge — food sits next to blister-pack and PVA film packagers. Capability copy is templated (“prototype to high volume,” “Kaizen capable”). No process-first IA (you cannot land on “US retort co-packers, SQF, MOQ < 10k”). Trust is for MRO buyers, not emerging CPG founders. Paid supplier ads distort ranking inside the directory.

## 5. Can we win SEO vs CoPack Connect?

**Yes, if the product is a public, process-sliced plant table. No, if we copy the RFQ login.**

Why CoPack Connect is beatable in search:

1. **They chose not to be a directory.** FAQ: you don’t scroll listings. Public pages are explainers + RFQ. Google rewards the page that answers “who are the HPP co-packers?” with names, cities, certs, MOQs — which they withhold.
2. **Process SERPs are owned by plants.** A directory that aggregates those plants (with permission or fair-use public facts) and adds comparison is a new SERP type. CoPack Connect’s hot-fill and retort URLs did not displace InnoMark / Berner / Universal Pure in this pass.
3. **CPA endorsement helps trust and plant recruitment, not content quality.** Thin parking-spaces and a Tabasco paragraph on a cookie-dough page are crawlable liabilities.
4. **Paid plant landing pages are few and sales-led.** Level 2 is only $595 — if we offer free, rich, process-tagged public profiles, plants have a reason to dual-list.
5. **Head term is a different war.** “Find a co-packer” is CoPack Connect / PartnerSlate / Wonnda / CPA. Do not make that URL 1. Make process URLs 1–10, then earn the head term later.

Why we can still lose:

- CPA + Melville distribution (Pack Expo, Fancy Food, State of the Industry) can out-PR a startup on the head query.
- If they turn parking-spaces into real dossiers and add public filters, the window closes.
- Domain age + association links will outrun a new domain on generic terms for 12–24 months.

**Win condition (SEO):** each process page = definition + when to use the process + **named US plants with state, certs, MOQ band, formats, FDA/USDA, allergen, and a “request intro” CTA.** Not an encyclopedia. Not an RFQ black box.

## 7. Go / no-go: search as the acquisition channel

### Verdict: **NO-GO as primary. GO as supporting (content + plant recruitment + GEO).**

Primary acquisition should be **LinkedIn (founder/ops + plant BD) + a newsletter**, with SEO as the public utility that makes both convert.

**Why search is not the engine:**

- Volumes for the money queries are **UNKNOWN** and, from SERP composition, look like classic **low-volume B2B** (plants rank themselves; directories barely appear except on the head term).
- The head term is an RFQ-platform brawl against CPA-endorsed CoPack Connect, PartnerSlate ($4M+ raised), Wonnda, and Keychain. A new domain will not win “find a co-packer” in year one.
- Process queries convert to **the plant**, not to a middleman, unless the directory is clearly better than Google’s ten blue plant sites (comparison table, MOQ, “who actually takes startups”).
- Buyer frequency is low (a brand finds a co-man once every few years). Newsletter + LinkedIn capture the *relationship*; search captures the *incident*.

**Why search is still worth doing (the 10 pages):**

- It is the only way to become the **citation layer** for AI/Google answers (“HPP co-packer US”) once plants are tabulated.
- It is a **plant-recruitment magnet**: BD teams Google their own process terms; a ranked HPP page that lists them (or omits them) is a sales conversation.
- CoPack Connect left the process URLs hollow. That is a 12–24 month window, not a forever moat.
- State extension lists prove Google already rewards **named-plant HTML**. Copy that structure nationally.

**Channel mix (recommended):**

| Channel | Role | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | Primary demand (brands) + supply (plants) | Buyer is a founder/COO/commercialization lead. CPA/Melville already work this graph. |
| Newsletter | Primary nurture + sponsorship revenue | Comps show 4k–40k is a real B2B F&B list; PMMI’s 27k is *plants*; Specialty Food SmartBrief 41k is *brands*. Own the intersection. |
| SEO process pages | Supporting + defensive | 10 pages above; public plant table CoPack Connect will not ship without cannibalizing RFQ. |
| SEO head term | Ignore until domain has the 10 pages + links | Do not spend the first content cycle on “find a co-packer.” |
| Pack Expo / Fancy Food / CPA adjacency | Plant supply | CoPack Connect was born there; dual-list plants rather than fight the endorsement. |

**Kill criteria (revisit in 6 months):** if a Keyword Planner/Ahrefs export later shows the 10 process terms are collectively **< a few hundred US searches/month** *and* pages attract no plant inbound, freeze SEO at maintenance and put 100% into LinkedIn/newsletter. Until that export exists, do not pretend the channel is large — and do not abandon the process pages that plants already vote for with their title tags.
