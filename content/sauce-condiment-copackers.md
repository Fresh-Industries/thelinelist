## What process your sauce actually needs

A sauce is not one process. Hot fill, cold fill, retort, and acidified kettle are different kill steps, different filings, and different plants.

**Hot fill.** Heated, filled hot, held, then cooled. Default ambient path for **high-acid** ketchup, hot sauce, many BBQ sauces and marinades. Not a retort shell.

**Acidified kettle.** Acid added so finished pH is **4.6 or below** — **acidified food** under 21 CFR 108/114. Needs a process authority letter and Form FDA 2541e. [The Spice Guy](https://saucecopackers.com/) will not quote without one. 100-gallon kettles are equipment; FAQ floor is **50 gallons per flavor**.

**Cold fill.** No hot-fill kill step. [Parish](https://parishfoods.com/pages/co-packing) hot-fills some sauces and cold-fills others. [Supreme](https://supremecuisine.com/bottling/) lists cold fill next to hot fill and retort.

**Retort.** Scheduled cook of the **already-closed** pack when pH **> 4.6**, aw **> 0.85**, hermetic, ambient — **LACF** (21 CFR 108/113). [Supreme](https://supremecuisine.com/bottling/) and [Stir](https://www.stirfoods.com/custom-sauce-manufacturer) publish retort separately. Do not send LACF to a hot-fill acid line.

## When this page is the wrong process

**pH will not hold at or below 4.6 ambient.** File a scheduled LACF process. Retort or aseptic — not a sauce hot-fill line.

**“Never cooked,” live culture, or HPP-only claims.** Heat destroys that story. Use the HPP page.

**Dairy.** Cream and cheese sauces are often low-acid. [Yoshida](https://yoshidafoods.com/inquiry-co-pack/hot-fill-inquiry/) (not in the table) refuses dairy because the Portland plant is Kosher Pareve. Ask for a dairy program and a process authority letter.

**Meat or poultry.** USDA FSIS vs FDA is not the pH table. Treat the USDA column as **unpublished** unless the plant states USDA-inspected or FDA-registered. Organic is *not* FSIS inspection.

**Alcohol and tin cans.** Plant-specific. Co-Packing Express: no potable alcohol, no tin cans.

## Acid vs low-acid (operator version)

Use the FDA definitions, then make the plant prove a **process authority letter for your formula**. A “we do sauces” page is not a filed schedule.

FDA: register and **file a scheduled process** per product, style, container, and method (**21 CFR 108**). **LACF:** pH **> 4.6**, aw **> 0.85** (excludes alcohol and tomatoes with pH **< 4.7**). **Acidified:** acid added to a low-acid food; finished pH **≤ 4.6**, aw **> 0.85**. Do not assume salsa is “just acid.”

| Class | Finished equilibrium pH | Water activity | What the plant must have | Typical sauce process |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Acid food (naturally acid) | 4.6 or below without added acid | usually > 0.85 | Thermal process that kills vegetative pathogens/spoilage organisms in the pack | Hot fill is common (many fruit sauces, some tomato) |
| Acidified food | 4.6 or below **after** acid is added | > 0.85 | Registration + filed schedule (Form FDA 2541e) under 21 CFR 108/114; pH as a critical factor | Hot fill / acidified kettle if it acidifies in time |
| LACF | **greater than 4.6** | > 0.85 | Registration + filed schedule under 21 CFR 108/113 | **Retort** or aseptic — not a standard hot-fill acid line |
| Refrigerated (not ambient) | any | any | Cold chain; LACF/AF filing often does not apply if retailed refrigerated | Cold fill, HPP, or hot fill then refrigerate |

**USDA vs FDA** is not the pH table. Supreme and Stir publish **FDA- and USDA-inspected** facilities. That is not a meat-sauce room map. Confirm jurisdiction before sampling.

### How to read the MOQ column

Published minimums are not comparable units. Parish and The Spice Guy quote **gallons per flavor/run**. Creative and Supreme quote **1,000 gallons**. Fischer **2,500 units/SKU**. El Pinto **10,000 units/year**. Onofrio **100 cases**. Motherlode (omitted) **400 gallons** first trial. Chelten, Stir, Tulkoff, Sonoma, Paradigm, Best Brand: **unpublished**.

Convert before you rank plants. A 50-gallon Spice Guy run is not Supreme’s 1,000-gallon minimum.

If MOQ is unpublished, assume nothing. Do not treat unpublished national houses as small-MOQ plants.

## Process decision (before you request an intro)

| Your SKU | First question | If yes | If no |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hot sauce, BBQ, ketchup, marinade, acid salsa | pH ≤ 4.6 in time? Process authority? | Hot fill / acidified kettle. Spice Guy 50 gal/flavor; Parish 25/50 gal; Creative / Supreme 1,000 gal; El Pinto hot-fill; Best Brand acidified/thermal; Onofrio 100 cases. | pH will not hold ambient → retort. Never heated → HPP. |
| Creamy dressing, aioli | Ambient or refrigerated? Dairy? | Stir, Tulkoff, Best Brand, Paradigm name dressings. Parish cold-fills some. Fischer: **no ambient-fill salad dressings**. | Refrigerated-only: not a shelf-stable hot-fill house. |
| Pasta / pizza / tomato | Tomato pH band (< 4.7) classified? | Chelten, Stir, Supreme, Creative, Sonoma, Parish, El Pinto (salsa) name these sauces. | Chunk + low acid → ask retort vs hot fill. |
| Cheese/cream sauce, bone broth, pH > 4.6 ambient | LACF? | Retort. Supreme and Stir publish retort. | Do not force hot fill. |
| Meat in the formula | USDA-FSIS or FDA? | Supreme and Stir state USDA inspection. Confirm the **room**. | Do not assume Chelten’s dressing line is a meat line. |
| Glass jar, first run under ~100 gal | Who publishes a small gallon floor? | Parish 25/50 gal; Spice Guy 50 gal/flavor; Onofrio **100 cases** (gallons unpublished). | Chelten / Stir / Tulkoff / Paradigm / Best Brand unpublished. Creative / Supreme **1,000 gal**. El Pinto **10,000 units/year**. |
| Pouch | Named sauce pouch? Hot-fill or retort? | Chelten 8–64 oz FS; Stir and Tulkoff pouches/sachets; Onofrio 8–128 oz. | Fischer **will not** pouch liquids. |

Bring to the first call: target pH and aw, dairy y/n, meat y/n, pack drawing, annual volume in **the plant’s unit**, organic/kosher need, and a process authority letter if you have one.

## Request an intro

If a plant in the table fits pH, format, and published minimum, we can **introduce you** — a named intro to a plant you already picked, not an AI match or blind RFQ. We will not email a plant until you name one and ask. Line time, allergen changeover, filing, and real MOQ still get decided by the plant.

If none fit (HPP-only, sub-startup volume, unpublished pack, dairy they refuse), say that instead of forcing a sauce house.
