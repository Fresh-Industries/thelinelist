# Co-packer vs Co-manufacturer vs Private Label vs Tolling

This is a glossary for CPG brand ops. It is not a plant census and not a legal textbook.

The “copacker vs co-manufacturer” SERP is definitional blogs — [The Fresh Factory](https://thefreshfactory.co/blog/cpg-manufacturing-models/), [LegalClarity](https://legalclarity.org/co-packer-vs-contract-manufacturer-which-do-you-need/), [Private Label Supply](https://privatelabelsupply.com/glossary/co-manufacturing). They argue the dictionary. US food plants use the words interchangeably on their own sites. The mistake that wastes a month is sending a refrigerated HPP juice to a hot-fill sauce house because you asked for a “co-packer.”

Search volume: **UNKNOWN**. Last verified **21 Aug 2026**. Unpublished MOQs stay unpublished.

## The four terms (food, not cosmetics)

Textbook split: a **co-packer** packages bulk product you already made; a **co-manufacturer / contract manufacturer** makes the product from your formula, then packs it; **private label** is the plant’s formula under your (or a retailer’s) brand; **tolling** is a fee to run *your* materials or *your* filled pack through *their* equipment.

Operator split: ignore the homepage noun. Ask who owns the formula, who buys the ingredients, and which **process** the line actually runs.

The blogs stop at “pack vs make.” In food, the next question is the **kill step**. Hot-fill sauce is not a retort pouch is not HPP juice. A stick-pack powder house is not a kettle.

## What these terms are not

**Not a broker.** A broker takes a cut to match you. CoPack Connect’s own FAQ says it is “neither a manufacturer nor a broker.” We are not one either. Request an intro to a named plant; we do not take a commission on the run.

**Not a CoPack Connect RFQ login.** That product is a logged-in AI match. You submit volume, format, and certs; manufacturers do not scroll a public list. Their [hot-fill](https://copackconnect.com/contract-packaging/hot-fill-bottling/) and [retorting](https://copackconnect.com/contract-packaging/retorting/) URLs still name **zero plants**. A glossary is not an RFQ.

**Not a 3PL.** A 3PL warehouses and moves finished goods. [3PL Hub’s co-manufacturer finder](https://www.3plhub.co/resources/tools/find-a-co-manufacturer) is a side product of a freight matcher. Cold storage next to an HPP vessel is a plant add-on, not the process.

**Not interchangeable with “white label.”** Fresh Factory treats white label as a CPG brand buying a catalog SKU (versus a retailer store brand). Same ownership problem as private label: you do not own the formula. We do not have a white-label page.

## Request an intro

If a plant on a process page matches your process, pack, and published floor, we can **introduce you**. That is a named intro to a plant you already picked — not an AI match, not a blind RFQ blast, and not a guarantee they will take the SKU.

We will not email a plant until you name one and ask for the intro. Line time, allergen changeover, and real MOQ still get decided by the plant.

If you do not know the process yet, say that in the request instead of asking us to “find a co-packer.”
