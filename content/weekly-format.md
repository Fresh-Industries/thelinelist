# Weekly format — The Line List

**Working name:** The Line List  
**Cadence:** Tuesday morning, America/Chicago  
**Audience:** CPG brand operators (founders, brand ops, commercialization). Not the PMMI/CPA plant-side list.  
**Length:** 400–700 words.  
**Source:** `/workspace/niche-research/copack-seo-teardown.md`

---

## Sections (every issue)

1. **The mismatch** — one process, MOQ, allergen, or cert problem (~120 words).  
2. **Who will actually run it** — named plants or plant types, public facts only, state, published MOQ or unpublished, link to the plant site. Never a fake AI match.  
3. **Process rule** — one decision (HPP vs hot-fill vs retort vs acidified vs aseptic).  
4. **One thing that changed** — plant claim, extension-list update, CoPack Connect/CPA, Pack Expo, FDA.  
5. **Reply ask** — process + target MOQ + state. We do not email plants unless asked.

---

## Sponsorship

One slot later (co-packer, packaging converter, or 3PL). **Do not invent a rate.**

Public analog only, and only as a comp, not our price: FoodBev 2026 kit weekly round-up **€2,500 / $2,800** at **8.3k** weekly subscribers.
